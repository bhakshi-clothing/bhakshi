Exchange & returns

Our polos and rugbys are cut relaxed on purpose. If the fit isn't what you wanted, we'll help you swap the size.

  • Size exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Pieces must be unworn, unwashed and have their original tags attached. Please send knits back folded, not on a hanger, so they keep their shape.
  • Exchanges are subject to stock. Because every style is made in a small run, your new size may already be sold out. If it is, we'll offer a different style from the drop or store credit.
  • To start an exchange, use the exchange form or message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and the size you need.
  • If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace it at no cost (or refund it if no replacement is available).
  • We don't offer refunds for change of mind; exchanges and store credit are available as described above.

Caring for your polo

Piqué, rib, waffle and heavy jersey all last longer with a little care. The label inside each piece has the final word; this is the short version.

  • Wash cold (30°C or below), inside out, with similar colours. Striped and contrast-collar pieces are best washed with darks for the first few washes.
  • No bleach and no harsh detergents — they fade stripes and yellow white collars.
  • Dry in the shade. Lay knits flat to dry so they don't stretch; polos can go on a broad hanger once they're nearly dry.
  • Reshape the collar while damp — smooth it flat and line up the points, and it will keep its shape.
  • Iron on low, inside out. Never iron directly over embroidery, raised script or appliqué.
  • Skip the tumble dryer; cotton knits can shrink and lose their shape in the heat.

Privacy

This website has no accounts, no database and no tracking pixels. Here is exactly what happens with your information:

  • Your order is sent by you, via WhatsApp. When you place an order or use a form, the details you entered (name, phone, address, items, optional email, gift flag and note) are put into a WhatsApp message that you send to us. Nothing is sent to any other server by this website.
  • Stored in your browser only. Your bag, wishlist, recently viewed items, size-finder answers, a saved delivery address (if you choose to save it) and your last order summary are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. You can clear them anytime by clearing your browser data.
  • Third-party services this site uses:
    • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of the request.
    • api.postalpincode.in — when you type a pincode at checkout, it's sent to this public service to fill in your city and state.
    • api.qrserver.com — after a UPI order, our UPI ID and the order amount are sent to this service to draw the payment QR code.
    • WhatsApp (Meta) — messages you send us are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • How we use what you send us: only to confirm, deliver and support your order. We share your name, phone and address with our courier partner for delivery. We never sell your data.
  • Want us to delete your order chat details? Message us and we'll remove them from our records, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

We release styles in numbered drops, each made in a fixed, limited run.
