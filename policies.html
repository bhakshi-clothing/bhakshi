02

Cancellation

  • You can cancel any order before it is dispatched — just message us on WhatsApp with your order ID.
  • If you paid by UPI, the full amount is refunded to the same UPI ID, usually within 5–7 business days.
  • Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can use our size exchange after delivery.
  • We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, the address is not serviceable, or we can't reach you to confirm it. Any payment made will be refunded in full.

03

Exchange & Returns

Getting the right fit matters to us. If a size doesn't work out, we'll help you swap it.

  • Size exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed and have their original tags attached.
  • To start an exchange, use the exchange form or message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and the size you need.
  • Exchanges depend on stock. If your size is unavailable, we'll offer another colour, item, or store credit.
  • If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace it at no cost.
  • Items bought on sale and innerwear are not eligible for exchange unless damaged or incorrect.
  • We don't offer refunds for change of mind; exchanges and store credit are available as described above.

04

Privacy

This website has no accounts, no database and no tracking pixels. Here is exactly what happens with your information:

  • Your order is sent by you, via WhatsApp. When you place an order or use a form, the details you entered (name, phone, address, items, optional email and note) are put into a WhatsApp message that you send to us. Nothing is sent to any other server by this website.
  • Stored in your browser only. Your cart, wishlist, recently viewed items, size-finder answers, a saved delivery address (if you choose to save it) and your last order summary are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. You can clear them anytime by clearing your browser data.
  • Third-party services this site uses:
    • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of the request.
    • api.postalpincode.in — when you type a pincode at checkout, it's sent to this public service to fill in your city and state.
    • api.qrserver.com — after a UPI order, our UPI ID and the order amount are sent to this service to draw the payment QR code.
    • WhatsApp (Meta) — messages you send us are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • How we use what you send us: only to confirm, deliver and support your order. We share your name, phone and address with our courier partner for delivery. We never sell your data.
  • Want us to delete your order chat details? Message us and we'll remove them from our records, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons.
