The craft

Made by hand, in small runs

Every BHAKSHI tee starts as heavyweight 240 GSM cotton and a drawing about everyday India — the monsoon, the four o’clock chai, the overnight train, the back of a highway truck.

Each print is pulled by hand, one screen and one colour at a time. Some are finished with embroidery or a brush. We make each design in a small numbered run, and when it’s gone, it’s gone.
