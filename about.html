01 / Who we are

Indian streets,
printed heavy.

We're a small label making the tees we couldn't find: heavy enough to hold their shape, cut boxy with dropped shoulders, and carrying graphics that come from here — monsoon evenings, the 4 PM chai break, overnight trains, truck art on the highway.

No seasons and no catalogue of hundreds of styles. Just drops: a few designs at a time, made properly, sold direct to you and confirmed one-on-one on WhatsApp.

02 / Why drops

Made in small runs.
On purpose.

(01)

Limited units

Every design is made in a fixed run, and the counter on each tee shows exactly how many are left. 09 / 40 means nine.

(02)

No endless restocks

When a design sells out, that's usually it. We'd rather make something new than reprint the same tee forever.

(03)

Less waste

Small runs mean we make close to what people actually buy — fewer unsold boxes and less dead stock sitting in a warehouse.

03 / How it's made

Five steps.
No shortcuts.

From a sketch to your door — every tee goes through the same five stops.

  1. 01

    Design

    Artwork starts as sketches of things we see every day, then gets redrawn until it holds up at full back-print size.

  2. 02

    Print

    Graphics go onto 240 GSM cotton — big back panels, small chest marks.

  3. 03

    Wash

    Each tee is bio-washed for a softer, lived-in feel from the very first wear.

  4. 04

    Check

    Every piece is checked for print, stitching and measurements before it's folded.

  5. 05

    Pack

    Folded, packed and sent your way, with the tracking link shared on WhatsApp.
