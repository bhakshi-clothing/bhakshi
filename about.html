BHAKSHI is a small Indian label making oversized tees — heavy cotton, quiet cuts and graphics drawn from everyday life here.

Who we are

We make the tees we couldn't find: heavy enough to hold their shape, cut boxy with dropped shoulders, and carrying graphics that come from here.

Monsoon evenings, the 4 PM cutting chai, overnight trains, the painted backs of highway trucks — small, familiar things, drawn carefully and printed onto heavyweight cotton.

No seasons and no catalogue of hundreds of styles. Just a few designs at a time, made properly, sold directly to you and confirmed one-on-one on WhatsApp.

Printed by hand in small runs

Every design is made in a fixed, numbered run — a few dozen pieces, not thousands.

Prints are pulled by hand, one colour at a time, then each tee is washed, checked for print, stitching and measurements, and folded before it leaves us. Working this way is slower, and the small differences from piece to piece are part of it.

Small runs also mean we make close to what people actually buy — less dead stock, fewer unsold boxes.

The techniques

A handful of ways we put a graphic onto cotton. Each tee lists the ones it uses, right under its name.

How a drop works

We release designs together, as a numbered drop.

  1. 01

    Drawn

    Each graphic starts as a sketch of something we see every day, then gets redrawn until it holds up at full back-print size.

  2. 02

    Made in a small run

    Every design is printed in a fixed, limited quantity on heavyweight cotton.

  3. 03

    Released together

    The designs go live at the same time as one drop. If there's a launch time, the shop shows a countdown until then.

  4. 04

    Gone when it's gone

    When a run sells out, that's usually it. We'd rather make something new than reprint the same tee forever.
