Our Story

Clothes for the everyday Indian man — made to be lived in, not saved for later.

BHAKSHI began with a simple frustration: good basics were either too expensive, too flimsy, or cut for someone else's body and someone else's weather. So we set out to make the pieces we actually wanted to wear — every day, in India.

What we stand for

Office shirt, weekend chinos. The same wardrobe, all week.

Why we exist

Fewer, better things.

Most men we know wear the same handful of pieces on repeat — a tee that fits right, a shirt that survives the commute, trousers that move when you do. We think those pieces deserve the most care, not the least.

So we focus on the essentials: cotton-rich fabrics that breathe through a Chennai afternoon or a Delhi summer, fits that work from 18 to 50, and colours that pair with everything already in your cupboard.

From a crisp Oxford for Monday's meeting to a cotton kurta for the festive season, every piece is designed to earn a permanent spot in your rotation.
